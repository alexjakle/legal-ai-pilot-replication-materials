# DEFENDANT TELLTALE CARDIAC SYSTEMS' ANSWER AND AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSES

Defendant Telltale Cardiac Systems ("Telltale"), through its counsel Kenge, Carboy & Vholes LLP, answers the Complaint filed by Plaintiff Dorothea Hartwell on March 4, 2025, as follows.

## Preliminary Statement

Telltale designs and manufactures implantable cardiac pacing leads, including the ArcLine AL-4120 bipolar active-fixation lead. Telltale sympathizes with Plaintiff's medical experience but denies that the AL-4120 was defectively designed, that its labeling was inadequate, or that any act or omission of Telltale caused her injuries.

## Responses to Numbered Allegations

1. Telltale admits that it is a corporation that designs, manufactures and markets implantable cardiac pacing leads. Telltale denies the remaining allegations of Paragraph 1.

2. Telltale lacks knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to Plaintiff's residence and age, and on that basis denies the allegations of Paragraph 2.

3. Telltale admits that venue is proper in Ashgrove County. Telltale denies that Plaintiff has stated a claim on which relief may be granted.

4. Telltale admits that a lead bearing model number AL-4120 was implanted in Plaintiff on or about March 19, 2019, at Westmarch Regional Hospital. Telltale lacks knowledge as to the surgical technique employed and therefore denies the allegations concerning the implant procedure.

5. Telltale admits that the AL-4120 was approved for distribution following the federal regulator's review of Telltale's premarket submission, and that the lead was accompanied by instructions for use. Telltale denies Plaintiff's characterization of those instructions.

6. Telltale admits, on information and belief, that Plaintiff underwent a routine device interrogation on November 8, 2022, and that a lead revision procedure was performed on November 15, 2022. Telltale denies that the lead was defective and denies all remaining allegations of Paragraph 6.

7. Telltale denies the allegations of Paragraphs 7 through 12, which assert that the AL-4120 conductor coil was unreasonably susceptible to fatigue fracture, that a safer alternative design was feasible, and that Telltale knew of a heightened fracture rate before Plaintiff's implant.

8. Telltale denies the allegations of Paragraphs 13 through 17, which assert that Telltale failed to warn implanting physicians of the risk of conductor fracture associated with placement near the clavicle and first rib.

9. Telltale lacks knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to Plaintiff's alleged medical expenses of $214,000, her alleged lost earnings, and her alleged pain and suffering, and therefore denies the allegations of Paragraphs 18 through 22.

10. Telltale denies that Plaintiff is entitled to any of the relief requested in her prayer, including the demand for compensatory damages in excess of $1,900,000 and for punitive damages.

11. Any allegation not expressly admitted is denied.

## Affirmative and Other Defenses

Telltale asserts the following defenses without assuming any burden of proof that the law places on Plaintiff. Telltale reserves the right to amend this Answer as discovery proceeds.

**First Defense (Failure to State a Claim).** The Complaint fails to state a claim on which relief can be granted, including as to punitive damages.

**Second Defense (Learned Intermediary).** Telltale's duty to warn ran to the implanting physician, who was adequately warned of the risks of conductor fracture through the instructions for use and related physician communications. Plaintiff's implanting physician exercised independent medical judgment in selecting the lead and the implant approach.

**Third Defense (Adequate Warnings).** The warnings and instructions accompanying the AL-4120 were adequate and complied with applicable regulatory requirements.

**Fourth Defense (State of the Art).** The design, testing, manufacture and labeling of the AL-4120 conformed to the state of the art and to generally recognized industry standards at the time the lead left Telltale's control.

**Fifth Defense (Regulatory Compliance).** The AL-4120 was designed, manufactured and labeled in compliance with the requirements of the federal regulator. Compliance supports a presumption that the product was not defective and, to the extent federal law so provides, preempts claims that would impose requirements different from or in addition to those requirements.

**Sixth Defense (No Feasible Safer Alternative).** No reasonable alternative design was available that would have prevented the claimed injury without impairing the lead's utility, safety or cost.

**Seventh Defense (Causation).** The conduct of Telltale was not a cause in fact or proximate cause of Plaintiff's alleged injuries. Plaintiff's lead fracture, if any, resulted from the implant technique, lead positioning, patient anatomy or patient activity.

**Eighth Defense (Intervening and Superseding Cause).** Plaintiff's alleged injuries were caused by the acts or omissions of third parties over whom Telltale had no control, including those involved in the implant and follow-up care.

**Ninth Defense (Comparative Fault).** To the extent Plaintiff failed to follow medical advice or to attend recommended follow-up visits, her recovery is barred or reduced in proportion to her fault.

**Tenth Defense (Failure to Mitigate).** Plaintiff failed to take reasonable steps to mitigate her alleged damages.

**Eleventh Defense (Statute of Limitations and Repose).** Some or all claims are barred by the applicable statutes of limitations or repose. Telltale will evaluate this defense once the date of Plaintiff's first notice of symptoms is established.

**Twelfth Defense (Misuse or Alteration).** To the extent the lead was subjected to forces, handling or positioning inconsistent with its labeling, Plaintiff's claims are barred or reduced.

**Thirteenth Defense (Setoff).** Telltale is entitled to a setoff for amounts paid by any collateral source or settling party to the extent permitted by law.

**Fourteenth Defense (Punitive Damages).** The Complaint pleads no facts showing conscious disregard of safety, and any award of punitive damages would violate Telltale's constitutional rights to due process.

**Fifteenth Defense (Reservation).** Telltale reserves the right to assert further defenses as discovery warrants.

## Prayer

Telltale requests that the Court enter judgment in its favor, dismiss the Complaint with prejudice, award Telltale its costs, and grant such further relief as is just.
