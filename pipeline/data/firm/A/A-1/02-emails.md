Gideon,

Thank you. We will respond formally within the thirty days, but I can give you our preliminary position on scope now so we can narrow the dispute.

The complaint concerns a single model, the AL-4120. Telltale will produce the design history file for that model and the testing that supported its release. It will not agree to produce files for every lead in the ArcLine family. Different models in the family have different conductor coil pitch and insulation thickness, and a request keyed to the whole family is not proportional to a case about one lead.

On complaints, we can agree to produce complaint and returned-product analysis records for the AL-4120 from its 2018 release through the date of Ms. Hartwell's revision on November 15, 2022. Reaching back to 2015 would sweep in products that were not on the market. Anything after the revision date is not relevant to what Telltale knew when the lead was implanted.

Regarding expert timing, a simultaneous exchange favors the party with the burden of proof, so we prefer sequential disclosure: plaintiff's reports first, ours sixty days later, then rebuttals. We also propose that expert depositions follow all fact depositions, including that of the treating cardiologist, Dr. Anselm Teague.

Priya
