# Firm store

- `firm-01` [pattern] Clients generally want a say before testifying experts are engaged. Expect to give the General Counsel candidate names and credentials, and sometimes prior-testimony notes, before engagement. Expect extra clearance for industry-practice or standards experts, and for expert reports where the client has a regulatory-consistency review. Build client review time into the expert schedule.
- `firm-02` [pattern] Settlement authority sits with the client, not the firm, and is tiered. Above a threshold, a further approval is needed (CFO sign-off, board written consent, or the General Counsel's written approval). Verbal indications are not enough. The firm recommends a range, and no offer or signal goes out until authority is confirmed. Flag early where approvals take lead time (e.g., about three weeks for a board consent), and confirm the current authority amount per matter.
- `firm-03` [pattern] Clients set structured reporting requirements. These include a recurring periodic report (monthly, or every six weeks), a call or short note after significant events, and an early initial case assessment (45 to 60 days). Several also require a phase budget within about 45 days. Formats are prescribed (e.g., one-page exposure summaries instead of narrative memos), and budget variances must be explained. Calendar these deadlines at intake.
- `firm-04` [pattern] All case communications go through a designated General Counsel, with a designated insurer or risk-management contact copied on coverage and exposure matters. Coverage questions go only to the named insurance contacts. Confirm the routing list at intake.
- `firm-05` [pattern] Clients restrict firm contact with their personnel and the outside world. Client employees and engineers are not contacted directly; access is arranged through the General Counsel or the client. There are no media or public statements, and press, regulator or customer inquiries are referred to the client. Witness offers are discussed with the client first.
- `firm-06` [pattern] Clients cap staffing and attendance, and prescribe billing. Typical limits are two or fewer lawyers or timekeepers at depositions or hearings, and a small core team. Billing is monthly, in a client-specified format.
- `firm-07` [pattern] Clients expect prompt notice of related incidents and preservation steps. Report other claims or incidents involving the same or related products promptly, and follow or recommend litigation holds and preservation.
