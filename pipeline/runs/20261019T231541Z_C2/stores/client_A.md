# Client store: Telltale Cardiac Systems

- `N412193.05` [fact] Telltale's complaint database showed 31 confirmed conductor fractures among about 22,000 ArcLine AL-4120 leads shipped (about 0.14 percent) as of the 2025 review. Engineering had not yet confirmed whether the rate stayed within the labeled range.
- `N412193.06` [fact] A 2020 internal note from a Telltale field engineer said returned AL-4120 leads showed wear from repeated bending near the pocket and suggested the design be revisited. As of October 2025 it had not been produced in discovery in a prior AL-4120 matter, and it is regarded as a sensitive document.
- `N412193.07` [fact] A 2021 Telltale trend report followed an internal quality inquiry into returned-lead fractures, called the Larkspur review. Plaintiffs may argue the report put Telltale on notice of a rising fracture rate and required updated warnings.
- `N412193.08` [fact] The AL-4120 instructions for use warn against medial subclavian puncture and describe lateral venous access as the preferred approach.
- `N412193.24` [position] Telltale settlement authority process: after the firm assesses a claim, it sends the General Counsel an assessment and recommended range. Authority above $500,000 needs sign-off from the CFO, Cormac Brightwater.
- `N412193.26` [fact] Telltale's product liability program carries a $750,000 self-insured retention per claim. Thaddeus Wren, the outside insurance liaison, coordinates with the excess carrier, and coverage positions may be discussed only with him or the General Counsel.
- `N412193.27` [position] Client instruction on experts: Telltale wants to be consulted before any testifying expert is engaged, with three names per discipline and a note on prior testimony. Its regulatory affairs group, led by Odalys Fennimore, must review every expert report before service, allowing ten business days, to check that nothing conflicts with what Telltale told the regulator. Reports may not go outside the firm and the experts until cleared.
- `N412193.30` [fact] Client reporting and handling requirements: a monthly written update and a call after significant events; at least five business days' notice before filing any motion; phase budgets with variance explained above ten percent; all communications to the General Counsel with a copy to Mr. Wren; no more than two timekeepers per deposition at Telltale's expense; and a biweekly call with Ms. Raghunathan in the early phase.
- `N412193.31` [fact] Telltale's working budget assumption on a product liability case was resolution or trial for under $500,000 in fees and costs combined, with an explanation wanted if the estimate goes higher. Because Telltale is in a regulatory inspection tied to its next-generation lead, it asks the firm to avoid public filings that characterize failure rates beyond what the case requires.
- `N412193.32` [fact] Client confidentiality and preservation instructions: contact with the media, treating physicians, or employees below vice-president level needs prior clearance, apart from the interviews the client set up. All AL-4120 and ArcLine-family documents must be preserved under a litigation hold. Design engineering and complaint-handling records (tracked in the Orrinwick database) are confidential, and a protective order must be proposed before producing them.
