# Client store: Telltale Cardiac Systems

- `N412193.24` [fact] Telltale settlement authority process: counsel sends an assessment and recommended range to General Counsel Marguerite Salvesen for the claims committee. Authority above $500,000 requires sign-off from CFO Cormac Brightwater.
- `N412193.26` [fact] Telltale requirements on experts: the client must be consulted before any testifying expert is engaged (three names per discipline, with prior testimony notes). Its regulatory affairs group, led by Odalys Fennimore, must review every expert report before service to check consistency with what Telltale told the regulator. The group needs ten business days, and reports may not be shared outside the firm and the experts until cleared.
- `N412193.28` [fact] Telltale reporting and handling requirements: a monthly written update and a call after significant events. An initial case assessment is due within 45 days of the client's engagement letter. Five business days' notice is required before any motion. Phase budgets are required, with variance explained over ten percent. All communications go to General Counsel Marguerite Salvesen with a copy to insurance liaison Thaddeus Wren. The firm should have a day-to-day contact, with a call every two weeks early on.
- `N412193.29` [fact] Telltale guidelines: no contact with media, treating physicians, or Telltale employees below vice president level without clearing it with the General Counsel. Preserve all relevant product documents (a litigation hold applies). No more than two timekeepers at a deposition are paid for. Invoices are monthly in task-based format. Design engineering and complaint-handling records are confidential, and a protective order should be proposed before producing anything.
- `N412193.30` [position] Telltale wants a vigorous but measured defense. Avoid public filings that characterize device failure rates further than the case requires, because Telltale is in a regulatory inspection tied to its next-generation lead.
- `N412193.31` [fact] Telltale's product liability program has a $750,000 self-insured retention per claim. Coverage matters go only to Thaddeus Wren or Marguerite Salvesen.
- `N412193.32` [fact] Telltale tracks complaint data in Orrinwick, its complaint-handling database.
- `N4fc817.21` [lesson] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale requires discussion with the General Counsel before any testifying expert is signed, and asks for fee quotes before engagement.
- `N4fc817.22` [fact] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale instruction letter from General Counsel Marguerite Salvesen. Reporting: a monthly written status report (max 3 pages) on the first business day; a call within 2 business days of any court ruling or key deposition; a full liability and damages assessment within 60 days; a week's notice before any non-emergency motion. Other instructions: direct communications to her, copy insurance liaison Thaddeus Wren on coverage or exposure matters, staff with no more than three lawyers, and observe the litigation hold.
- `N4fc817.23` [position] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale does not want material produced in one matter to appear in any other matter. Obtain a protective order limiting use to the lawsuit before any production. Do not share strategy or work product from one matter with other Telltale counsel unless the General Counsel asks.
- `N4fc817.24` [position] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale settlement approach: listen if plaintiff raises resolution but give no number, and make no offer in any form until plaintiff's treating cardiologist has been deposed. After that deposition the firm sends an assessment and recommended range. Authority above $500,000 needs approval from CFO Cormac Brightwater, which the General Counsel will arrange.
- `N4fc817.28` [lesson] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale asks to approve any proposed mediator and to have confidentiality confirmed, so the mediation cannot be used against it in other cases, before the firm agrees to mediate.
- `N4fc817.29` [position] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale requires that no defense expert report be served until its regulatory affairs group (under Odalys Fennimore) has compared it with Telltale's regulatory submissions for inconsistency. Allow two weeks for review, and route any comments through counsel to the expert, not directly.
- `N4fc817.31` [fact] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale budget requirements: a phase-by-phase budget within 45 days; prior approval for any spend above the approved phase budget; and a written explanation to the General Counsel for any projection above the client's working assumption.
- `N4fc817.33` [position] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale rules on witnesses and contacts: contact Telltale employees only through the General Counsel's office, and do not approach treating physicians or other providers without her approval. Tell her in advance of any corporate-representative designation and let her see the topics. Do not offer anyone from the manufacturing floor without speaking to her.
- `N4fc817.34` [lesson] (tags: wall:A-3) Telltale wants to see any draft stipulation before it is sent to opposing counsel. Clear drafts with the General Counsel first.
