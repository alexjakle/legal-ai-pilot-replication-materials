## The Case

Ms. Okafor alleges that the lid of her TT-6200 turned open while the cooker was under pressure and that she was badly burned. We take any burn injury seriously and we are sorry for her pain. We also stand behind the TT-6200 lid system, which went through independent laboratory testing before launch. Our focus is to understand what happened in her kitchen and what condition the unit is in. You should also know that we have promised our largest retail partners advance warning before we pull any product from sale, so tell me at once if anything in this case could lead there.

## Sequence of Work

Our practice with cases like this is to try mediation before the parties get into expert work. Expert discovery is expensive, it hardens positions, and in our experience most of these cases are better resolved once the facts are known but before the experts have been paid and committed. Please plan the schedule accordingly: get the inspection done, exchange the core documents, and set a mediation before anyone designates an expert. If plaintiff's counsel presses for an expert schedule first, tell her we want a mediation date in the order, and let me know if she objects.

You may retain a consulting engineer now to attend the inspection; that is not what I am describing. I just do not want testifying experts disclosed or deposed until a mediation attempt has been made.

The page should show the current exposure as a range in dollars, the status in a few lines, upcoming dates, and any decision you need from me. I do not want narrative memos on the case. If something needs more explanation, call me. I would like a page each month and after any event that changes the range, such as an inspection result or a ruling.

Please send the first one by the end of May.
