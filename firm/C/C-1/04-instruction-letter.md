**Re: Estate of Lindqvist v. Joad Axle Works — Instructions to Counsel**

Dear Cornelius,

Thank you for agreeing to represent Joad in this wrongful death action. The suit arrived last week. Please treat this letter as our instructions for the defense, and share it with the team that will be working on the file.

## Background

Mr. Lindqvist's death in the June 2022 rollover is a tragedy, and we do not take it lightly. Our engineering department has reviewed the information available so far and does not believe the JX-9 axle failed because of its design. We do not yet know whether the spindle separated before or during the rollover, and we want that question answered by proper examination before we form a view on liability. Our engineers will cooperate with your team. One background point: Joad changed its spindle forging supplier in 2020 after an internal audit, and I ask that you not discuss the reason with anyone outside your case team.

## Handling Our Test Data

Joad's internal test data, including the fatigue series from 2017, represent years of work and are valuable to our competitors. If the plaintiff asks us to agree that these records are genuine as a matter of course, the answer is no, unless there is a protective order in place first. We will not stipulate to the authenticity of our internal test data without one. Insist on a protective order with a tier limiting access to outside counsel and experts. We will produce what the court requires, but not before the order is signed.

## Settlement and Authority

Our insurer, Cobaltridge Indemnity, has pre-approved authority for settlement up to $1,500,000. If discussions ever go beyond that point, you may not make or accept any offer without written sign-off from our board of directors, which meets monthly and can act in writing between meetings if the chair approves. Please give me enough notice. I need about three weeks to get a board consent properly prepared. Do not rely on a verbal indication from any director or officer. If an offer would fall within the insurer's tier, check with me before you convey it, because the insurer will want to be involved.

## Experts

I want to review the names and credentials of all proposed testifying experts before you engage them. Please also tell me whether you plan to use anyone to speak to industry practice. I would prefer that our design testimony stays focused on this axle. Joad has people who have served on standards bodies, and I am glad to help you identify candidates.

## Reporting

- A written report every six weeks, with a call after significant events.
- A first case assessment within 60 days, including a range of exposure and a probability of an adverse result.
- Copy the insurer's claims handler, Perpetua Wallace, on anything that bears on coverage, and send her the monthly invoice summary.
- A short note after every deposition within five business days.

## Standing Guidelines

1. All case communications should come to me. Do not contact current Joad engineers or production staff directly; I will arrange access and preparation. Our supplier-qualification program is called Corbelmark, and you should understand how it works before any discussion with a supplier.
2. A litigation hold went to our departments on June 15, 2023. Please send me your own preservation recommendations, especially for the 2017 test lab.
3. The fractured spindle and the tractor are in a storage facility not under our control. Move quickly to secure a joint inspection protocol, and send me notice of any proposed testing at least ten days in advance.
4. No public statements. Refer any inquiry from the media, from regulators or from customers to me.
5. Limit attendance at depositions to one lawyer from your firm and one from the insurer.
6. Invoices go to my office monthly and to Ms. Wallace in summary. Rates follow the master services agreement of March 2022.
7. Alert me immediately if you learn of other incidents involving JX-series spindles.

## Witnesses and Facts

Our chief axle engineer, Bertil Oakhurst, ran the 2017 test program and will be a central witness. I would like your team to interview him early and to keep a tight circle around who sees the test files. Our sales manager for the tractor-builder account can discuss how the JX-9 was specified and sold. Neither should be put forward for testimony until you and I have discussed it.

## Budget

Please prepare a phase budget within 45 days. I expect that a wrongful death case with a metallurgical dispute will cost in the range of $700,000 to $1,000,000 through trial, and I would like to know where you think we will land. Expert and testing costs will be most of the variance. I would like quotes for any testing before it is commissioned, and I would like to know who is paying for the shared lab work.

## Insurance Coverage

Our policy has a retention of $400,000 and an aggregate limit that we are watching closely because of two other open claims. Ms. Wallace will tell you if the carrier reserves its rights, and I will want to hear the same day.
