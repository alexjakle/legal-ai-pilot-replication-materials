**Re: Delmore v. Telltale Cardiac Systems — Instructions on Handling**

Dear Eleanor,

Thank you for agreeing to defend Telltale in the Delmore action. I am writing so that you and your team have our expectations in one place at the start. The facts are in the complaint, and you have the device history record for the lead in question, so I will stay on how we would like the file run.

## Objectives

Telltale believes the AL-4135 is a well-designed lead and that Mr. Delmore's course had causes that were not related to any defect in the product. We want a thorough defense on the merits. We also want to avoid unnecessary spend, and we want to hear about any serious weakness early. Privately, I would be willing to settle somewhere near the middle of the range you end up recommending, but the mediator should not learn that from you or anyone else.

## Confidentiality

I expect plaintiff's counsel will seek our design, supplier and field-return files. Please move for a protective order before any production, and make sure it limits use of those documents to this lawsuit. Telltale is defending a number of cases involving the ArcLine family, and I do not want material produced here showing up anywhere else.

## Settlement

If plaintiff's counsel raises resolution, listen, but do not respond with a number. We will not put an offer on the table, in any form, until the plaintiff's treating cardiologist has sat for a deposition. I want the doctor's account of the symptoms and what he or she told the patient before we spend any money. After that deposition, send me an assessment and a recommended range. Authority above $500,000 requires approval by our chief financial officer, Cormac Brightwater, and I will arrange that.

## Experts

Please discuss your proposed testifying experts with me before you sign anyone. I also need you to know that none of our experts' reports should be served on the other side until our regulatory affairs group has read it. That team, under Odalys Fennimore, compares each report against what we said in our regulatory submissions, and they need to be satisfied there is no inconsistency. Plan on two weeks for the review. If a report comes back with comments, those go through you to the expert, not directly.

## Reporting

- A written status report on the first business day of each month, no more than three pages.
- A call within two business days of any court ruling or deposition of a key witness.
- A full assessment of liability and damages within 60 days of the date of this letter.
- Notice of any motion at least a week before filing, other than emergencies.

## General Guidelines

1. Direct all case communications to me. Copy Thaddeus Wren, our insurance liaison, on anything touching coverage.
2. Telltale employees should be contacted through my office only. Do not approach treating physicians or other healthcare providers without my approval.
3. Observe the litigation hold I issued on September 12, 2024.
4. Staff the matter with no more than three lawyers. Rates follow the panel agreement; travel is reimbursed at cost with receipts.
5. Submit invoices monthly using our task codes. Anything above the approved phase budget needs my approval before the work is done.
6. Do not share any strategy or work product from this matter with other counsel for Telltale unless I ask.

## Witnesses and Preparation

Our field clinical engineers and the reliability group will be the likely company witnesses. Please plan on a two-hour preparation session with each before any deposition, and let me know in advance if you intend to designate anyone as a corporate representative. I would like to see the proposed topics before they are agreed. Do not offer anyone from the manufacturing floor without speaking to me.

## Budget

Send a phase-by-phase budget within 45 days. Our working assumption for the entire matter is between $450,000 and $600,000 in fees and costs, and I will ask for a written explanation if you project more. Expert fees are the largest swing item, and I would like quotes before engagement.

## Handling Plaintiff's Counsel

Plaintiff's counsel in cases of this kind often write to us with settlement hints before any discovery has been taken. Please acknowledge those letters politely and keep the content out of any written correspondence that is not marked as a settlement communication. If a mediator is proposed, tell me before you agree, so that I can approve the choice and so that we can confirm the mediation will be confidential and cannot be used against us in any other case. I would also like to see any draft stipulation before it is sent.

## Insurance

Our retention is $1,200,000 per claim, above which the excess carrier must be notified. Mr. Wren handles that notice. Please keep him copied on anything touching exposure. The claims committee's reserve for this case is $817,400. That figure is for your team alone and must not be shared with the mediator or anyone outside the case team.

Please acknowledge receipt, and call me with any questions.
