**Re: Hartwell v. Telltale Cardiac Systems — Engagement Instructions**

Dear Walter,

Thank you for taking the Hartwell matter. This letter sets out how we would like the case handled, what we expect by way of reporting, and the guidelines that apply to all of our product liability matters. Please share it with whoever on your team is working on the file.

## The Matter

Ms. Hartwell alleges that the ArcLine AL-4120 lead implanted in March 2019 fractured and required surgical revision in November 2022. We have pulled the device history record for her lead and our quality team will give your associate access this week. We do not believe the lead was defective. The AL-4120 has a long field record, and our own complaint data supports that. Complaint files and returns are tracked in Orrinwick, our complaint-handling database, and the quality team can pull from it for you. We want a vigorous but measured defense. The company is also in the middle of a regulatory inspection tied to our next-generation lead, so please avoid public filings that characterize failure rates any further than the case requires.

Please assign one senior associate as the day-to-day contact. I understand that will be Priya Raghunathan. I would like to speak with her once every two weeks in the early phase.

## Settlement

I want to be direct about one thing. Telltale will not make any settlement offer in this case until Ms. Hartwell's treating cardiologist has been deposed and we have read the transcript. I know plaintiff's counsel will push for early numbers. Do not engage on figures, and do not signal willingness to pay, until that testimony is in the record. Once it is, send me an assessment and a recommended range, and I will take it to our claims committee. Anything above $500,000 in authority needs sign-off from our chief financial officer, Cormac Brightwater.

## Experts

We will want to be consulted on the selection of any testifying expert before an engagement is signed. Please send me three names for each discipline, with a short note on prior testimony. In addition, our regulatory affairs group must see every expert report before anything is served. This is not a formality. The group, led by Odalys Fennimore, checks that nothing in a report conflicts with what we told the regulator in the original submission and in later reporting. Allow ten business days for their review and build that time into your schedule. Reports should not be shared with anyone outside your firm and the experts until they have cleared that review.

## Reporting

- A short written update every month, and a call after any significant event such as a ruling or deposition.
- An initial case assessment within 45 days of this letter, with exposure estimated as a range.
- Advance notice, at least five business days, before any motion is filed.
- Budgets for each phase, with variance explained when you exceed them by more than ten percent.

## Standing Guidelines

1. All communications about the case should come to me, with a copy to our outside insurance liaison, Thaddeus Wren.
2. Do not communicate with the media, with treating physicians, or with Telltale employees below the vice president level without clearing it with me first, apart from the interviews described below.
3. Preserve every document related to the AL-4120 and the ArcLine family. I have issued a litigation hold internally and will send you a copy.
4. Please staff the case leanly. We do not pay for more than two timekeepers at any deposition.
5. Invoices go to my office monthly in the task-based format we use for all panel firms. Rates are as agreed in the panel agreement.
6. Keep our design engineering and complaint-handling records confidential. If plaintiff seeks them, propose a protective order before producing anything.

## Documents and Witnesses

Our quality and engineering staff will be your main source of facts. I will make three people available for interviews in April: our director of reliability engineering, the quality engineer who handled the device history review, and the clinical specialist who covers the implanting hospital. Please prepare short outlines in advance so that we limit the time they spend away from their work. Telltale's design history file for the AL-4120 is large, and I would rather you tell me what you need than ask for everything.

## Budget

Please send me a phase budget within 30 days covering pleadings, written discovery, fact depositions, expert work and dispositive motions, and a separate trial budget at the end of expert discovery. Our working assumption is that the case should be resolved or tried for less than $500,000 in fees and costs combined, and I will want an explanation if you see it going higher. We will consider alternatives to hourly billing for the trial phase if you want to propose one.

## Insurance

Telltale's product liability program carries a self-insured retention of $750,000 per claim. Mr. Wren will coordinate with the excess carrier. Do not discuss coverage positions with anyone but him or me.

## Closing

Telltale takes the safety of its devices seriously, and we also take seriously the cost of litigating a case we believe is meritless. I trust the firm to find the balance. Please confirm receipt and call me with any questions.
