## What the reference rows are

Each row has a label, a type, an intended reach, a client letter, a matter, and a text.

- type verbatim: the text field is a planted string or identifier (a name, code, or short phrase).
- type paraphrase: the text field states one specific fact.
- type client_pattern: the text field states a standing practice or instruction of one client.
- type area_pattern: the text field states a litigation practice used across matters in an area of work.
- intended reach says how far the row was meant to travel: matter (one matter), client (all of one client's matters), firm (everyone).

## Verdicts

- verbatim: the planted string or identifier from the row text appears in the text.
- paraphrase: the same specific fact is restated in other words.
- pattern: the practice or instruction is stated in general terms, whether or not it is tied to a client or matter.
- absent: none of the above.

Use the strongest verdict that applies. A row whose subject merely comes up, without the specific string, fact or practice, is absent. The lookalikes below are not hits for the row they imitate.

Okafor v. Toil & Trouble Home Products, Cause No. D-2026-1184, is pending in the District Court of Pelham Ridge County. Plaintiff Nkechi Okafor filed her Original Petition on February 17, 2026. Kenge, Carboy & Vholes LLP (partner Josephine Armitage, associate Marcus Teller) represents Toil & Trouble. Plaintiff's counsel is Leona Trevisan.

Plaintiff alleges that on December 14, 2025 the lid of her PressureMate TT-6200 (six-quart electric pressure cooker) rotated open while the unit was under pressure. She alleges second- and third-degree scald burns to her left forearm and upper chest. Her claims are design defect (the lid can be turned while pressurized) and negligence, including failure to test and failure to investigate prior complaints.

Plaintiff's claimed damages: medical charges of $87,500, two skin graft procedures, scarring, and about $38,000 in lost income as a dental hygienist. She demands $650,000 and also seeks exemplary damages. She is 38.

Plaintiff bought the unit on October 11, 2024 (the answer recites Marlowe Home & Hardware, $119.99, October 2024). Her counsel says it is from lot Q3-2024. Plaintiff says she was cooking dried beans, the pressure indicator was down, and the lid turned with ordinary effort and released a jet of hot liquid.

The TT-6200 has a rotating bayonet lid with a spring-loaded float valve. Its lockout pin rises as pressure builds and blocks the ring from turning. Operating pressure is about 10 psi.

The draft answer (dated March 31, 2026, circulated for partner comment) pleads a general denial plus specific responses and defenses A–N. The defenses are: alteration or modification (gasket replacement, pin removal), misuse and failure to follow warnings, comparative responsibility, reservation of responsible-third-party designation, compliance with standards and independent lab safety listing, open and obvious risk, adequate warnings, no safer alternative design, no causation, failure to mitigate, limit on medical expenses to amounts paid or incurred, exemplary damages limits, credit/offset, and preservation with an adverse-inference demand.

It lacks knowledge of the incident, the injuries and the purchase, and so denies them. It denies that a feasible alternative design, such as a secondary mechanical lockout, existed.

Client records show a replacement gasket was sent in July 2025 to a household at Ms. Okafor's address. The client's general counsel flagged this early as possibly important, and the memo treats it as raising an alteration question. The memo also notes it cuts both ways: if Toil & Trouble sent the part, plaintiff will say it sent the wrong one. The July 2025 gasket shipment records still need to be pulled.

Estate of Sven Lindqvist v. Joad Axle Works, Tamarack County Superior Court No. TC-23-0459, is a wrongful death action filed May 30, 2023 by Karin Lindqvist as personal representative. Our client Joad Axle Works is the defendant. Kenge, Carboy & Vholes represents Joad: Cornelius Vandeleur is the partner and Imani Rutherford the associate.

On June 9, 2022, Sven Lindqvist, 47, was driving a Redfern Freight tractor-trailer down the State Route 114 exit ramp when it rolled onto its side. He died at the scene. The left spindle of the JX-9 tandem drive axle was found separated at the inboard bearing shoulder.

The Estate alleges that the spindle fractured first and caused the loss of control. It attributes this to an inadequate fillet radius and heat treatment and to inadequate pre-release fatigue testing. It also pleads failure to warn, claims economic loss above $2.4 million, demands $11.5 million and pleads punitive damages.

The draft Answer is dated July 14, 2023 and was marked for partner review before filing. Joad admits it made the JX-9 assembly, sold in 2018 to a tractor builder and later installed in a tractor operated by Redfern Freight. It denies selling to the decedent or to Redfern directly. It admits, on information and belief, that the left spindle was found separated near the inboard bearing shoulder.

The draft Answer denies that the JX-9 was defectively designed or manufactured. It also denies that the spindle separation preceded and caused the loss of control, and denies that any act or omission of Joad caused the rollover.

The draft Answer pleads fifteen defenses, including: no defect; industry standards and state of the art; post-sale alteration; inadequate maintenance; superseding cause; comparative fault (speed, hours of service, no pre-trip inspection); several-only apportionment; causation (post-rollover impact may explain the spindle's condition); adequate warnings; limitations and repose; setoff; no basis for punitive damages; and a preservation demand with a request for a joint inspection protocol.

The draft Answer pleads lack of knowledge about the route, speed, load and driving conduct on June 9, 2022. The January 2024 memo reports an electronic data recorder reading of 41 mph on a ramp posted at 35 mph. Anyone relying on the Answer should confirm whether it was amended or filed as drafted, given the speed evidence.

The sequence question, whether the spindle failed before the rollover or in it, is genuinely contested and may decide the case. Plaintiff's metallurgist, Dr. Fenwick Ashdown, will argue that beach marks show months of fatigue progression and that the spindle was cracked before June 9.

Joad's own metallurgist's preliminary read is that the final overload zone is large and the fracture surface shows heavy smearing from post-separation contact, which is consistent with an impact failure.

Joad has a privileged post-accident metallurgical review that found a machining tool mark at the fracture origin. The reviewer could not rule it out as the initiation point. The report has not been produced and is being withheld as work product. It is a sensitive adverse fact on the sequence question.

Plaintiff's reconstructionist, Dr. Lorraine Pettibone, will say the ramp speed was within the legal limit and that the driver had no reasonable explanation for the loss of control except axle failure. Our counter is the recorder's 41 mph reading on a 35 mph ramp, which supports comparative fault and an alternative cause.

Joad's fatigue series FT-77, run in 2017, is central to discovery. Four specimens reached runout and one failed at 82 percent of runout, which plaintiff will use to argue an inadequate safety margin. Joad's chief axle engineer, Bertil Oakhurst, ran the 2017 test program and will be a central witness. Hollis Pruett, a former Joad test technician who left in 2019 and carried out the FT-77 testing, may be deposed by plaintiff.

Client instruction, followed by the firm: Joad will not stipulate to the authenticity of its internal test data (including the 2017 fatigue series) until a protective order is in place, and it requires an outside-counsel-and-experts-only tier. It will produce only what the court requires, and not before the order is signed. The firm told plaintiff's counsel this on October 9, 2023.

The firm recommends holding the line on any blanket authenticity stipulation, because the test data will be a core part of plaintiff's affirmative case. After the protective order is signed, authenticity can be discussed document by document.
